What Is Macimorelin

Growth hormone (GH) levels increase gradually during childhood, followed by a rapid surge at the time of puberty (1). It continues to be released approximately till the age of 30, after which the levels of growth hormone (GH) decline significantly.

An anomaly in the release of growth hormone (GH) either at birth (congenital) or developed later (acquired) can lead to significant changes such as slow growth, incomplete sexual development, and low stamina.

Macimorelin acetate, a synthetic growth hormone secretagogue (GHS), is chemically defined as D-Tryptophanamide, 2-methylalanyl-N-[(1R)-1-(formylamino)-2-(1H-indol-3-yl)ethyl]-acetate (2).

The pharmacokinetic study of macimorelin suggests a similar affinity to the growth hormone secretagogue receptor (GHSr) like the endogenous ligand (3).

Therefore, this peptide binds with the specific receptors to cause an enhanced release of growth hormone (GH).

How Does Macimorelin Work

Our body produces ghrelin, which binds with growth hormone secretagogue receptors (GHSr), particularly GHS-R1a. This binding triggers the release of growth hormone (GH) from the central nervous system so that growth hormone (GH) levels are at the optimal limit.

Macimorelin, more commonly known as Macrilen, acts as an agonist for the growth hormone secretagogue receptor (GHS-R1a).

As this compound mimics the action of the endogenous ligand ghrelin, it stimulates the hypothalamus and anterior pituitary gland to cause the pulsatile release of growth hormone (GH) into the circulatory system.

Therefore, growth hormone (GH) travels to its target sites and acts in various ways to bring changes in the human body.

Benefits Of Macrimorelin

Clinical trials suggest that macrimorelin has excellent bioavailability and stability for the growth hormone secretagogue receptors (GHS-R1a) (3).

This has led to doctors approving the use of this peptide as a test for the adult growth hormone deficiency (AGHD) over the insulin tolerance test (ITT). The main reason is that, unlike the insulin tolerance test (ITT), it significantly minimizes the risk of developing hypoglycemia (4).

The macimorelin induced release of growth hormone (GH) offers a wide array of prospective benefits. It can act as a potential treatment in children for the problems associated with growth, such as dwarfism.

In adults, the release of growth hormone (GH) may cause a reduction in body fat while increasing the lean body mass at the same time.

It also possesses the ability to minimize the risk of developing metabolic disorders. It can help develop glucose tolerance. It can also act as a treatment for dyslipidemia.

Macimorelin is also hypothesized to improve the cardiovascular health of individuals by keeping the heart parameters within the normal range.

How To Use Macimorelin

Since macimorelin is used as a diagnostic test for growth hormone deficiency, it is advised to be taken in front of your doctor either at a hospital or clinic.

Once the doctor has decided on your dosage guidelines, make sure to not eat or drink for at least 8 hours prior to consuming macimorelin (5).

This peptide is available in the market as granules (powder form) that needs to be reconstituted first. Mix the granules with water, and then within the span of 30 seconds, make sure to drink the dissolved liquid.

Your doctor then draws your blood sample after 30, 45, 60, and 90 minutes to analyze the levels of growth hormone in your circulation.
