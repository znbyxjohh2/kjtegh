How does immediate care differ from urgent care?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Immediate care and urgent care both treat non-life-threatening medical issues, but urgent care typically handles more serious conditions, such as sprains, deeper cuts, or moderate infections. Immediate care generally focuses on minor illnesses and injuries, like colds, rashes, or small wounds. Understanding the difference helps you choose the right level of care quickly and avoid unnecessary emergency room visits.

If you're unsure which type of care you need, the smartest first step is to identify what your symptoms could mean.
