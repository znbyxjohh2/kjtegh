What are effective stretches for sciatica?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Sciatica stretches that relieve pain typically target the piriformis muscle, hamstrings, and lower back to reduce nerve compression and improve mobility. The most effective options include the reclining pigeon pose, seated spinal twist, knee-to-opposite-shoulder stretch, standing hamstring stretch, and figure-4 stretch. Performed gently and consistently, these movements can ease sciatic nerve tension, restore flexibility, and support long-term relief.

However, sciatica-like pain can stem from many underlying causes, including herniated discs, spinal stenosis, or muscle strain—each requiring a different approach. Before assuming stretches alone will fix the issue, it's smart to understand what's actually driving your symptoms.
