# Sizing Up Your HDL

Just when you thought you had a handle on the acronyms busily clogging (and then clearing) your arteries, we now have five more for you to memorize: HDL2b, HDL2a, HDL3a, HDL3b, and HDL3c. These are the names of different sizes of HDL (2b being the largest, 3c the smallest). It turns out that large HDL particles are kinder to your arteries than small particles. In fact, small particles of HDL may actually increase rather than decrease the amount of artery-clogging plaque (just as small LDL particles do). "In the future, more and more of the story of cholesterol will be in the size of the particles, in addition to the overall amount of HDL," says Chris Slentz, Ph.D., an exercise physiologist at Duke University medical center.

In a recent study published in the New England Journal of Medicine, Slentz and his colleagues looked at whether cardiovascular exercise could increase HDL2b and HDL2a, and if so, the amount of exercise needed. Their finding: Simply jogging 20 miles a week elevates the larger HDL by 19 percent. If you're interested in having the size of your HDL particles measured, direct your doctor to liposcience.com, the Web site of the company that provides the test. "This is the next generation of cholesterol management," says Seth Baum, M.D., a cardiologist in Boca Raton, Florida, and author of The Total Guide to a Healthy Heart. "A lot of doctors don't know about it, but they should."
