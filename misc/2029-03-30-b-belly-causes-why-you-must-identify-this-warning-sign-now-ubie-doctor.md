What causes the formation of a 'B belly' in some individuals?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

A "B belly" refers to an abdominal shape resembling the letter "B," where the stomach appears divided into two rounded sections by a horizontal crease. While often linked to weight distribution, excess visceral fat, or postpartum changes, a B-shaped belly can also be associated with Prune Belly Syndrome, a rare congenital condition affecting the abdominal muscles, urinary tract, and occasionally other organ systems.

Key causes of a B belly include:

Explanation

Prune Belly Syndrome is a rare disorder that mainly affects males. It is characterized by three main features: poor development of the belly muscles, problems with the urinary system, and undescended testicles in males. These issues can lead to a belly that looks wrinkly or like a 'B shape'.

Key Points:

Belly Muscles: The muscles in the belly area are weak or missing, which can make the belly look wrinkled in children and appear as a smooth "pot" belly in adults.

Urinary System Problems: The urinary tract, which includes the kidneys and bladder, may not work properly. This can lead to infections and other issues.
