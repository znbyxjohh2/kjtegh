# Watch Dr. Pimple Popper Squeeze a Bowlful of Oatmeal From This Cyst

• In a new Instagram video, Dr. Sandra Lee, MD—aka Dr. Pimple Popper—pops a patient's cyst.• The dermatologist likens the cyst's contents to oatmeal.• Warning: This graphic cyst extraction video just might ruin your favorite breakfast food.

If oatmeal is your favorite breakfast food, you might want to switch to toast after watching Dr. Pimple Popper's newest Instagram video.

In a new video posted Wednesday, dermatologist and TLC star Dr. Sandra Lee, MD—better known as the famed Dr. Pimple Popper—squeezes a whole lotta whitish gunk out of a patient's cyst. The cyst looks fairly small from the outside—it's nothing like this massive unicorn cyst, for instance—but believe us, there is PLENTY of pus inside, just waiting to rear its lumpy head.

"I inCYST you POP out of bed with some #oatmeal," Lee wrote in the pun-filled caption alongside the video.

It's gross, but she's absolutely right: The contents do look exactly like oatmeal. Particularly the kind of oatmeal you leave in the microwave for too long, and that proceeds to cascade over the lip of the bowl in a thick, sticky mess. When Lee is done squeezing out all the "oatmeal," she folds it up in some gauze and whisks it away.

"Why must you compare these with food?! 😂" one viewer wrote in the comments section.
