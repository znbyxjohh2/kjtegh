Alas, no. Series creator Peter Morgan has confirmed that the show's sixth season will be its last. "I'm proud of having got to the end and having done what I said I would do," he told Tudum. "The queen was both a very ordinary person, and a completely unique and remarkable person."

Morgan explained that it was never his intention for the narrative of the show to catch up entirely with the present day, and that he "wanted to remain a careful distance" from more recent events including the marriage of Prince Harry to Meghan Markle, the death of Queen Elizabeth, and the coronation of King Charles.

[Getting Season](https://pepperedx.com/dkfxw9pm/)

"If the show is a train, the train has moved at a speed of a decade a season," he said. "So over the six seasons, we’ve explored around 60 years... I think we all thought six seasons was perfect, two seasons for each queen."

"It's also really satisfying to look at it as a whole and realizing how far we have all come," he added. "I loved it. I gave everything to it, I don't think I left anything on the table."

The real-life passing of Queen Elizabeth during production lent an additional sense of finality to Season 6, executive producer Suzanne Mackie explained. “We couldn’t help but reflect that in the emotional landscape of the [final] episode," she said. "It was influenced and altered, and I’m very glad we did because it feels like a proper end to our show."
