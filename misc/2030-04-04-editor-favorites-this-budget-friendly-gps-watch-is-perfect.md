When I started taking running a little more seriously, the first thing I decided I needed was a GPS watch. I was getting tired of bringing my bulky phone with me, and not to mention, I wasn’t 100 percent sure the app I was using to log my miles was 100 percent accurate.

As someone who started their career only fairly recently, I don’t have a ton of money to shell out on something expensive. So after poking around online a bit for something that wouldn’t make me cry the next time I looked at my bank account, I came across Timex’s Ironman GPS Watch for just under $100 ($99.95, Buy It Here), which fit into my budget perfectly.
