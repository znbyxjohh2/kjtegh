In a new YouTube video, Dr. Pimple Popper–aka, dermatologist and TLC host Dr. Sandra Lee, MD—works on a unique cluster of blackheads located around a man's eye. Specifically, these blackheads are known as solar comedones, or "favre racouchot." They frequently appear on areas of the skin that have been exposed to sunlight for an extreme amount of time, according to DermNet. Heavy smoking can also play a role. In the clip, Dr. Pimple Popper uses her comedone extractor to safely and gently squeeze the blackheads free, occasionally pausing to open them up with a scalpel-like instrument.

As with normal blackheads, the dark color has more to do with the comedones undergoing the process of oxidation. When the sebum inside of a blackhead is exposed to the air, it becomes oxidized and changes color. As for preventative measures, the prescription here is pretty obvious: Wear sunscreen, especially if you're fair-skinned and prone to burns, and of course, avoid tanning beds.
