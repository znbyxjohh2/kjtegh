Explanation

[Doctor's Note](https://innate-signal.jcesqc8.com/)

Shingles near the eye, known as herpes zoster ophthalmicus, is more serious than shingles in other areas because it can affect the eye and the nerves around it. This can lead to problems like eye pain, redness, and swelling. If not treated, it can cause vision problems and even blindness.

Why It Is Serious:

Eye Damage: The virus can infect the eye itself, causing pain and redness.

Vision Problems: It can lead to blurred vision or even loss of vision.

Nerve Damage: The virus can affect the nerves around the eye, causing long-term pain.

What To Do:

See a Doctor Quickly: Early treatment can help prevent serious problems.

Medication: Antiviral medicines can help reduce symptoms and protect the eye.

If you're experiencing symptoms near your eye and aren't sure if they're related to shingles, you can check your symptoms with a free online assessment to help determine whether you need urgent medical care.

Taking quick action is important to protect your eyesight if you have shingles near the eye.
