What might cause coughing and vomiting in adults?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Coughing followed by vomiting in adults is often a symptom of pertussis (whooping cough), a highly contagious bacterial infection marked by violent, uncontrollable coughing fits. These intense spasms can be so forceful they trigger vomiting, exhaustion, or a distinctive "whooping" sound when inhaling. While pertussis is a common culprit, cough-induced vomiting can also point to other conditions such as post-nasal drip, GERD, asthma, or respiratory infections—making an accurate assessment important.

Because symptoms overlap across many conditions, identifying the true cause on your own can be difficult.
