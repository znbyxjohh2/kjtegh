About this lesson

Stop missing treatment opportunities and learn to prioritize correctly. This facial assessment and prioritization course teaches Dr. Sopena's standardized 6-point method for comprehensive patient evaluation.

You’ll learn how to educate patients who demand treatments that aren't their priority. Watch real assessment identifying upper lip projection and chin as priorities while ruling out unnecessary forehead work.

Things you’ll learn

The 6-point assessment method, which covers shape, expressions, proportions, subunits, skin quality, and asymmetry.

How to prioritize treatments across multiple sessions.

Profile analysis using the nose-to-chin line to identify lip and chin projection issues that frontal views might miss.

Why facial subunits (nose and lips) are critical for low-budget patients.
