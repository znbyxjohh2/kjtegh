I had a surprisingly strong squat workout today. The medium grip overhead squat and power cleans served as a great warm-up to the squats. The overhead squat movement serves as a great warm-up for any type of workout, and like the Stick-up, it looks easy but is difficult, especially for those with limited shoulder mobility from bad posture and work environments. Start with a broomstick till you get good form.
