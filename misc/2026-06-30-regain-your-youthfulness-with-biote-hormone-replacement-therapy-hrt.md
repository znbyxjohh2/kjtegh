As we age, our bodies produce fewer hormones each year. By our mid-to-late 30s, the effects of low hormones begin to negatively affect our everyday lives. In men, testosterone levels decline 1-2% per year and human growth hormone continuously decreases. This is called andropause and typically results in a loss of libido (sex drive), an increase in abdominal fat, a decrease in lean body mass, fatigue, insomnia (inability to sleep), and low energy levels.
