A weak urine stream in males is often caused by benign prostatic hyperplasia (BPH), a condition where the prostate gland is enlarged. Treatment options vary based on the severity of symptoms and may include:

Lifestyle Changes: These can include reducing fluid intake before bedtime, limiting caffeine and alcohol, and regular physical activity to improve symptoms.

Medications: Alpha-blockers and 5-alpha-reductase inhibitors are common medicines that help relax the muscles of the prostate and bladder or shrink the prostate, respectively.

Surgery: In more severe cases, surgical procedures like transurethral resection of the prostate (TURP) may be considered to remove part of the prostate and improve urine flow.
