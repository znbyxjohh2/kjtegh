# The 2026 New Year's Fitness Center

Welcome to 2026!

WE’VE REACHED 2026! And with the turning of the calendar, we’re all offered the opportunity to reassess ourselves and our routines. You might focus on self-improvement at home, productivity at work, improving mental health—but most of all, people use the new year to reconsider their fitness routines. You can take this moment to be the starting point for your commitment to new healthy habits, or, if you're in the midst of a consistent fitness practice that makes you happy, a renewal for another trip around the sun following the same plan.
