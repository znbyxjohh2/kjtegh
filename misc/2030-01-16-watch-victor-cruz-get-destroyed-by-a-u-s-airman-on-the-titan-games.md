Victor Cruz might have retired from the NFL after six seasons as wide receiver with the New York Giants (a tenure which included one Super Bowl win), but he's stayed in champion shape — as he has been demonstrating on this season of NBC's The Titan Games. The show, hosted by none other than The Rock, pits elite athletes from all over the world against each other in a larger-than-life competition that puts their strength, speed, and endurance to the test.

"I'm extremely competitive. I don't know what 'take it easy' means, I'm a guy that likes to go full force," Cruz said during last night's episode. "I'm a Super Bowl champion, and now I'm going to be a Titan Games champion." He added that his experience in the world of professional football has taught him to always be prepared and bring his A-game, "both physically and mentally."

Last night saw Cruz face off against U.S. Air Force captain Noah Palicia in the epic Mount Olympus challenge, an assault course of giant proportions. "This mountain will test even the bravest of athletes," said The Rock. "Trust me, Noah and Victor, you both will be tested."

The two athletes started off on fairly equal footing, racing through the first few obstacles before both hitting the uphill log lift, a challenge which commentators speculated might be difficult for Palicia's upper body strength. But this is where Palicia gained his lead on Cruz, an advantage which the NFL champ never quite managed to claw back. Palicia then sped ahead to obstacles like the cage crawl and 300-pound chain pull, and ultimately won the entire Mount Olympus challenge.

But don't worry — Cruz will still be participating in The Titan Games semi-finals later this season.
