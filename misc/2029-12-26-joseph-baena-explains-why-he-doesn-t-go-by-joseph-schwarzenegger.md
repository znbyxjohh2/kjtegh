# Joseph Baena Explains Why He Doesn't Go by Joseph Schwarzenegger

Joseph Baena has always appeared to have a close relationship with his father, Arnold Schwarzenegger, but despite beginning his own career as an actor, has never used the instantly recognizable surname to help himself get ahead. In a recent conversation with Loni Love and Morgan Stewart on E! Online, he explained why.

"There's a few different variables in that," said Baena. "But that's just not my main priority at the moment, so I'm just focused on building myself up and building my career in acting and real estate and just focusing on that right now."

One area where Baena very much does invite comparisons to his famous father, however, is over on his Instagram page, where he regularly shows off his gains from Gold's Gym in Venice by flexing and recreating some of Schwarzenegger's most iconic poses from back in his heyday as a professional bodybuilder, during which he won the prestigious title of Mr. Olympia no fewer than seven times in a row.

Fitness and weightlifting are passions that Schwarzenegger and Baena both share; they frequently work out together and Baena has described his dad as "the best training partner in the world" who pushes him and never lets him "cheat" in an exercise.
