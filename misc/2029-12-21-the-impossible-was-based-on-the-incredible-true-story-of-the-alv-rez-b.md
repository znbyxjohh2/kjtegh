# 'The Impossible' Was Based on the Incredible True Story of the Alvárez-Belón Family

2012’s The Impossible arrived on Netflix this month. The film tells the story of a family struggling to survive in the aftermath of the 2004 Indian Ocean earthquake and tsunami, which made landfall and ripped across several countries, including Thailand, where the family had been vacationing during the Christmas holidays.

The destruction was caused by an underground earthquake, where one ocean plate slips below another. The two plates in this case were the Indian and Australian tectonic plates, comprising a 900-mile fault line. The earthquake—a 9.1 magnitude, and so one of the largest ever recorded—triggered a tsunami by causing the ocean floor to suddenly rise by 40 meters.

By the time the tsunami ended, eight hours later, some 200,000 people had lost their lives. The event remains the deadliest tsunami in recorded history.

The Impossible captures the moment the tsunami made landfall in Thailand—the second location after Sumatra, which was hit first and where almost 100,000 people were killed instantly. When the tsunami reached Thailand over an hour later, waves were still traveling at 500 mph. Nearly 5,400 people in Thailand would lose their lives, including 2,000 foreign tourists.

Among the tourists who would survive were María Belón, Enrique Alvárez, and their three children Lucas, Simón, and Tomás. The Impossible tells the real-life survival story of this family.

Who are the Alvárez-Belón family?

Belón and Alvárez spent Christmas of 2004 in Thailand, staying at the Orchid Resort Hotel. As in the film, the family was at the hotel pool when the tsunami made landfall. Belón, who is a doctor, was watching her three children play with their father.

“I was facing the sea and saw a huge black wall,” she said in a 2017 interview. “I didn’t think it was the sea. I thought it was a black wall coming to get us.” The hotel was then swept underwater.

Belón was able to stay close to her eldest son, and the two of them emerged from the waves clinging to a tree. She was then brought to safety by a local Thai man. She was certain her husband and two other sons had been killed.

But Alvárez had the two boys had survived. The family miraculously reunited later at a hospital.

Where is the family now?

Belón continues to work as a doctor as well as an advocate for survivors still recovering from the tsunami. Alvárez works with refugees arriving in Greece. In an interview several years ago, Belón said the family gathers on a beach each December 26 to remember. “No matter where we all are, this is a commitment we have forever. We need to be close to the souls [of the dead].”

All three boys have also committed to a life of service.

The eldest son, Lucas (played by Tom Holland) went on to study medicine at University College London. This past year, he worked the frontline of the COVID-19 pandemic, helping patients in London.
