Switching to a birth control pill that contains drospirenone may help reduce bloating. Drospirenone is a unique progestin with mild diuretic properties, meaning it helps the body release excess water and sodium. Unlike other progestins that can cause fluid retention, drospirenone-based pills (such as Yaz or Yasmin) may ease bloating, breast tenderness, and swelling linked to hormonal birth control.

If bloating or other symptoms are affecting your daily life, it's worth understanding the possible causes before making a change. Every body reacts differently to hormones, and bloating can also stem from digestive, dietary, or other underlying issues.
