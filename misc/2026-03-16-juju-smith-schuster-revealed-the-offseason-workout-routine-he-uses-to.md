# Juju Smith-Schuster Revealed the Offseason Workout Routine He Uses to Stay Fit

With the NFL season set to officially start on September 10, 2020, players have not been sleeping on their workouts during quarantine. Everyone from JJ Watt, Marquise 'Hollywood' Brown, Cam Newton and James Conner have been sharing the workouts they've been doing to stay in shape for the upcoming season. And JuJu Smith-Schuster, wide receiver for the Pittsburgh Steelers is no exception. He shared his entire workout on his YouTube channel in his trainer Corey Calliet's garage, and it's intense. "It's about to be a crazy workout," Smith-Schuster says.

His warmup starts out easy with some resistance band walks, then he moves to a combination move of a squat to overhead press followed by an inchworm. Next, he's on to renegade rows. "We're just getting started. Oh my god," he says. "I hate when he does shoulders!"

Caillet loves it already. "You don't have to be in a big sports training center with nice, fancy machines and sh*t. We be in my garage. I train world-class athletes in my garage, just like that." The workout starts with spot sprints followed by pushups with arm extensions, then back to sprints. After that, Smith-Schuster hits a fan bike.

"This is the hardest, bro. Top two hardest thing we o in here. I don't know what it is," he says before he starts peddling. "We just getting started and it feels like a sauna in here."

He does some extended leg medicine ball presses, as Calliet yells, "Work for it!"

"If I'm training an athlete, we'll do bodybuilder stuff to build an actual physique that's going to be strong, that's going to help muscle be really good and conditioned and have a lot of endurance," Calliet says.
