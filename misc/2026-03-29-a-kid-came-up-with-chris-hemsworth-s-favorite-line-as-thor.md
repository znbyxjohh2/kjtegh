# A Kid Came Up With Chris Hemsworth’s Favorite Line As Thor

With Avengers: Endgame set to be Chris Hemsworth's seventh feature turn playing Thor, he's had quite a bit of experience playing the god of thunder. So when asked on Jimmy Kimmel Live! for the favorite line he's delivered, Hemsworth had one come to mind right away, and it was one that was not only crushed on the silver screen, but had a tender story behind it's origin as well.

It's no secret that 2017's Thor: Ragnarok, under the guidance of director Taika Waititi, leaned all the way into the comedic timing that Hemsworth had sparingly but strongly put on display in previous installments of the franchise. But no one knew the exact origin of one of that film's most memorable lines.

[Kid Came Up](https://repair-cascade.jcesqc8.com/)

"When we were filming Ragnarok, we had a young kid on set who was unwell at the time, and had come to visit. We were shooting the scene with Hulk and Thor in the arena, and it was right when I was talking to Hulk, and I was saying 'Oh my god, it's you! Where have you been?'" Hemsworth explained. "He was on the sideline, he was like, 'Why don't you say, when you look up at Loki in the crowd, say 'He's a friend from work'?'"

"A kid gave us that line, and it became one of the best lines in the movie," Hemsworth said.

[Hemsworth Favorite Line As](https://calmvector.dns0663.com/)

Scarlett Johansson also revealed her favorite line as Black Widow, which she delivered during Iron Man 2 re-shoots.

"I'm just bad at techie jargon," she said. "I'm terrible at it."

She took a moment to remember the line, remembering that her character was set at a computer screen, and overseeing some action. She got herself together before dialing back into character and giving it a stab.

[Chris Hemsworth](https://barrierwatch.bbsxyxy.com/blog/9942907459.html)

[Line As Thor](https://reboundworks.escortskhi.com/)

"Bogey is converging on the Oracle pavilion. Lock and load, the fight's coming to you," she re-claimed, staring right into the camera. "So bad. I think it got cut. I think, actually, [director] Jon Favreau is saying the line, and I'm just mouthing the words. That's how bad it was."

Two different favorite lines from two different distinct experiences. Marvel really does have something for everyone, huh?
