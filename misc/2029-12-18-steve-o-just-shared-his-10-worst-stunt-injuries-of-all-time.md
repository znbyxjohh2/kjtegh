# Steve-O Just Shared His 10 Worst Stunt Injuries of All-Time

In the latest update to his YouTube channel, stuntman and former 'Jackass' star Steve-O put together the most epic video of his worst stunt injuries of all-time, and most of them are guaranteed to make you cringe. "I’ve been looking forward to putting this video together for a long time... talk about a wild ride!" he wrote in the video's description. Naturally, Steve-O starts the video from the least gnarly of his injuries (which, to be honest, are still pretty gnarly), and makes his way to the most.

10) Roof Jump into Hot Tub

"When I jumped off that roof into the hot tub, I didn't just hit the bottom of it—my butt clipped the bench," says Steve-O. "Then, later that night, my butt became pretty deformed. And as the days went by, it just became more deformed, more bruised, more painful. Thank God I got away with that one, because if you mess with your spine, that's not good."

[Worst Stunt](https://allpepay.com/blog/7910109543.html)

9) Balance Beam Fall into Burning Coals

"Burns are the worst," Steve-O declares, explaining that the idea for the stunt was for him to jump into a bed of coals after being knocked off a balance beam by flaming balls, but because the warehouse owner didn't want his floors damaged, metal sheets were added underneath the coals, creating a "frying pan." When he got knocked off, it was bad. "That was the first time I had to go to the hospital after I got sober, so I had no painkillers or anything. Just messed up burns on my butt," he says.

8) A 'Legendary' Night in Miami

This one wasn't a stunt, exactly, but it was still crazy. "Chris Pontius and I got alllll f*cked up. By the end of the night, Pontius was in a psychiatric ward because he was found butt naked sleeping on the roof of a parked car," Steve-O says. "And I was walking around with my toe not even connected to my foot, it was so dislocated it was just floating out on its own and my foot was broken." The next night, he had to get carried out on stage to present an award at the Latin America VMA's, and he still hadn't been to the hospital.

7) Jumping Off Van in a Trash Can

"I was launched off roofs, off of waterfalls, down stairs, into TV sets, you name it. But one of those stunts went bad," he says. "The wheel got jammed up, I fell over and landed on my collar bone. I didn't even go to the hospital for a few days. But sure enough, when I did, it showed a break."

6) Tubing Accident

This stunt required his first proper surgery on his lower leg after he was dragged on a tube behind a boat, and his leg hit something in the water.

5) Fire-Breathing Back Flip

[Shared His Worst Stunt](https://hostdefense-journal.escortskhi.com/blog/8381192976.html)

"One night at a backyard keg party, I decided to have somebody blow a fireball off my head and then I would do a fire-breathing back flip off all in one sequence. So I got the dude to blow it from the side, and when he blew it, it went straight into my face," Steve-O says. "So with my whole head on fire I do this fire-breathing back flip, which I came up short on. So I landed on my knees and I'm flailing trying to put it out. I remember I had all this skin on my face rolled up like a joint. It was missing all kinds of skin, and I thought 'I. AM. F*CKED.'" He went to the hospital and they scraped off the burnt skin. "It was the most painful shit I had been through, and for the next 6 months I couldn't be in the sun," he says.

4. Riding a Bell Hop Cart Down Stairs

For Jackass 2, Steve-O recreated a stunt he had previously done with 50 Cent where he rode in a bell hop cart down a set of stairs. "They shove the thing down the stairs, and I land on the bell hop cart," says Steve-O. "Somehow, man, I knew nothing was okay, dude."

[Stunt Injuries](https://metabolic-horizon.bbsxyxy.com/blog/9060931129.html)

He went to the hospital, and it turns out he fractured his vertebra. "A very minor fracture, but a broken back," he says. "I was out of filming for months. That's why I had so little footage in Jackass 2 because I was just laid up with a broken back."

3) Balcony Jump

"Way back in the '90s when I was just a homeless kid with a dream, I used to throw myself off of balconies," he says. At a keg party where he was drunk and high, he spun off the balcony, and his face hit the concrete first. "I'm just laying there, face-down on the concrete, a pool of blood just growing around my head. Everybody thought I was dead," he says. "I had a broken wrist, fractured skull, 7 broken teeth, and 10 stitches in my chin. It was a f*ckin' mess."

2) Porta-Potty Skateboard Jump

This stunt involved Steve-O standing on the top of a porta-potty while a car crashed into it as he jumped off and tried to land on his skateboard. He had 3 porta potties to nail it, and on the last one he was determined to stick it. But the stunt destroyed his ankle. "It was going sideways. I knew it was awful right away," he says. "I go to the hospital and it is surgery right away. They put 11 screws and a plate into my ankle." He got through it without painkillers, too.
