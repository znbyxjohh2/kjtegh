# Here’s How Much YouTuber KSI Made from that Logan Paul Fight

Last year, as a mark of millennial success, English YouTuber KSI used his internet stardom to break into amateur boxing. After challenging fellow YouTuber Jake Paul to a bout, KSI ended up fighting Jake Paul's controversial brother Logan Paul, twice, and that second bout brought in close to $3.5 million and 17 million YouTube views. That fight became the largest YouTube streaming event ever. (Boy, we’ve come a long way since the “Evolution of Dance.”)

[KSI Made from](https://huanle3.com/5igkn3kd2/)

KSI (born Olajide Williams Olatunji) first climbed to the top of British YouTube after posting FIFA video game commentary to his channel. The 26-year old has since mixed in comedic and blog-styled videos as well as raps. Last year he signed a record deal and his first album featured a track with American rapper Rick Ross. (His Youtube channel includes weekly posts and some 20 million subscribers).

[Here How](https://mimi567.com/6i9imr0i/)

With so much viewer and subscriber success, you might wonder just how much the 26-year-old rakes in through YouTube, i.e., his day job. His first paycheck, he says—which came from Machinima, a now-defunct online entertainment network—was $100, which he spent on an Armani blazer. “There’s a track called ‘Game Over’ by Tinchy Stryder, and he’s wearing this Armani blazer,” KSI remembers. “And I thought: SO. SICK. I was like: I have to get that Armani Blazer.”

[that Logan Paul](https://collagen-atelier.bbsxyxy.com/blog/5783645676.html)

Since then KSI’s subscriber base has exploded. The YouTuber reportedly nets around $5 million a year, more than enough to drop on ridiculous impulse buys—like a $500,000 Dragon Ball Z chain. No regrets. KSI also says he owns $10 million worth of real-estate in England. “Money can’t buy you happiness,” he jokes, “but it can definitely rent happiness.”

KSI’s most financially savvy move he conducted last year when he challenged the Paul brothers to an amateur boxing event. “I knew that from a business point o f view, me calling out one of the Paul brothers made sense,” KSI explains. “I wanted to fight Jake first so I could beat Jake in England and fight Logan in America. But Jake Paul, he’s a pussy. He didn’t come anywhere near me. And Logan was man enough to say ‘yes.’”

KSI says the main goal for him to capture the Paul brothers’ American audience. Already a smash in England, KSI wanted to familiarize American audiences with his name.

[from that Logan Paul](https://weight-signal.sunsimiaosd.com/blog/1540462157.html)

But the fight did even more for the Brit.

According to Forbes, both KSI and Paul earned $900,000 each from the fight. KSI also points out that the fight earned more pay-per-view buyers than the majority of non-Mayweather HBO and Showtime fights. His second fight with Paul sold more views than Anthony Joshua’s recent bout with Andy Ruiz Jr. “For two YouTubers to come out of the woodwork and just smash the boxing scene like we did, yeah, unbelievable,” KSI says.

He now has hopes of turning pro.
