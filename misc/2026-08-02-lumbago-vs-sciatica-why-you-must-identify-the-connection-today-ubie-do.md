Lumbago is a general term for lower back pain, while sciatica refers to pain that radiates from the lower back down through the buttock and leg, typically along the sciatic nerve. The two conditions are closely related: lumbago often appears as an early symptom of sciatica, and many people with sciatica also experience lumbago. However, not all lower back pain is caused by sciatica—it can stem from muscle strain, poor posture, or disc issues.

Explanation

Lumbago and sciatica are two conditions that affect the lower back, but they are not the same thing. Lumbago is a general term used to describe pain in the lower back. This pain can be due to many causes, such as muscle strain or arthritis.

Sciatica, on the other hand, refers to pain that travels from the lower back down through the buttocks and into one or both legs. This happens when something, like a herniated disc, presses on the sciatic nerve.

How They Are Related:

Cause: Lumbago can be a symptom of sciatica if the lower back pain is caused by pressure on the sciatic nerve.

Symptoms: While lumbago is just in the lower back, sciatica involves pain that moves down the leg.
