# The Rock Just Gave His Own Truck Away to a Deserving Fan

Dwayne "The Rock" Johnson has a reputation not just as one of the hardest working people in Hollywood, but also one of the nicest. He just helped himself hold onto that title by giving back to one of his fans, who he believed deserved some "joy".

[Just Gave His Own](https://memorycircuits.hbsnzj.com/)

[Truck Away](https://bioresearch.wiki/8fmo4gd/)

In a recent Instagram video which has gone viral, Johnson pays a visit to the unsuspecting Oscar Rodriguez, a personal trainer and Navy veteran, at a screening of his new movie Red Notice. He explains that he wanted to do something special for him, as reward and thanks for everything he does in the community, which includes taking care of his 75-year-old mother, being a leader in his church, and providing support and meals for women affected by domestic violence.

[Away to](https://mimi567.com/9urid8u/)

Johnson's first thought was to gift Rodriguez with his own Porsche Taycan, one of the vehicles he drives in Red Notice—but the car manufacturer said no to his request, and so he comes up with a pretty great backup plan, and hands Rodriguez the keys to his own custom Ford Raptor truck.

"I'm just speechless," says Rodriguez. "I did not know this was even going to happen. This is such a blessing... feel so encouraged right now, so uplifted... I can't tell you how many times I've seen you in this truck!"

"You do a lot of good for people, brother," says Johnson. "The gym, the church, your mom, women. Women who need that support, who are going through it."

Once Rodriguez has driven off in the Raptor, Johnson says it was an "honor" to bring some joy to a man who does so much for other people. And with his act of kindness complete, the only thing left for him to do is figure out how he's getting home without his ride.
