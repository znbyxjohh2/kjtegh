Like a growing number of actors, Zac Efron isn't afraid of physically transforming himself for a movie. This can mean sporting a new look—he grew a moustache for the upcoming factually-based drama The Greatest Beer Run Ever, and who can forget that bizarre barnet and beard combo in The Beach Bum?—but Efron is better known for sculpting his physique while getting ready for a role.

"It's definitely fun because you get to put everything on the line in terms of your work ethic and seeing how far you can push it," the actor told People in 2019, comparing his physical preparation for each role to an "Olympic event" before clarifying that he doesn't believe it is a sustainable approach to fitness: "It’s a finite period of time, but I would not consider that a healthy or normal way to live everyday life."
