If there’s one man in the NFL who knows how to enjoy his offseason, it’s Tom Brady.

The 40-year-old NFL quarterback ate the first strawberry of his life and also chugged a beer while on The Late Show with Stephen Colbert Monday night. Who are you, Tom, and what have you done with the man who proclaims avocado ice cream a delectable cheat meal?

Brady appeared on Colbert’s show to promote his book, The TB12 Method, which outlines his famously insane nutrition principles designed to keep a person playing professional football well into their 80s. The talk show host couldn’t help but bring up a proclamation Brady made back in September 2016, that he had never eaten a strawberry, and subsequently pounced on the opportunity to create history.

Brady, the relentlessly fierce competitor he his, accepted Colbert’s challenge to try a strawberry when the host presented him with a heaping container of the fruit.

“Not that bad,” Brady said. “I did it. Mom, are you proud of me?”

Sensing Brady may just be game for just about anything, Colbert turned the conversation to the indulgences Brady missed since committing to a diet of more or less water, electrolytes, and raw vegetables.
