# Sly Stallone's 'Headbanger' Pullup Is the Move You Need to Try ASAP

They say you can't teach an old dog new tricks. No one tell Sylvester Stallone, whose workouts continue to push the envelope even as he enters into his seventh decade.

The 71-year-old actor, currently training to step into the ring for the eighth time as Rocky Balboa in the upcoming Creed 2, has been gracing his fans with some impressive Instagram posts of his training regimen.

This new pullup variation is definitely something he didn't do back in 1976 when the Italian Stallion hit the big screen for the first time. In an Instagram video posted Tuesday, Stallone performs a pullup variation that works his arms and his core, as the move also includes an L-sit (although we can't see if someone was spotting his legs offscreen).

Sly wrote that he copied the move from trainer Jeff Cavaliere, whose Instagram feed is filled with functional strength training techniques that break from the old gym standards you've been doing for years. Cavaliere calls this particular variation a "headbanger" pullup, which he demonstrated on his own profile last week.

The headbanger helps to develop straight arm scapular strength, Cavaliere wrote, which is essential for deadlifts and performing more standard pullups for volume.

To perform the move, approach the bar as you would for a standard pullup with an overhand grip. Lift yourself up until your head is at bar height, simultaneously raising your legs into an L-sit position. Extend your arms and press back toward the bar until your forehead nearly touches, keeping your body balanced.

If you're itching to add a headbanger to your arsenal but can't quite pull it off yet, never fear. Cavaliere suggests that you can start off by looping a resistance band around the bar for assisted reps.
