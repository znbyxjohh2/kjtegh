3. How Much Protein Do Seniors Need?

The Recommended Dietary Allowance (RDA) for protein is 0.8 grams per kilogram of body weight. However, research suggests seniors may benefit from higher intake:

Standard RDA: 0.8 g/kg/day

Optimized for Seniors: 1.0–1.2 g/kg/day

Recovery or Illness: Up to 1.5 g/kg/day (under medical supervision)

6. Safety Considerations

A protein-rich reset and any gut-focused therapy require careful planning:

Hydration: Increase water intake to help kidneys process extra protein.

Kidney Function: Seniors with preexisting kidney disease should consult a doctor before increasing protein.

Balanced Nutrition: Don't sacrifice fruits, vegetables, whole grains, and healthy fats.

Gradual Changes: Introduce new foods and therapies slowly to monitor tolerance.

Professional Guidance: Work with a registered dietitian, doctor, or geriatric specialist.

9. When to Speak to a Doctor

Even well-planned resets can uncover underlying issues. Contact your physician if you notice:

Sudden weight loss or gain

Severe digestive discomfort

Signs of dehydration (e.g., dark urine, dizziness)

Unexplained muscle cramps or weakness

For anything life threatening or seriously concerning, please speak to a doctor immediately.
