# Paddy’s Pub Takes Over Dunder Mifflin For Season 14 of ‘Always Sunny’

[Pub Takes Over](https://huanle3.com/xwgfiijsbb/)

The Gang are heading from Paddy's Pub to Dunder Mifflin. With shooting underway on the 14th season of FX comedy series It's Always Sunny in Philadelphia, the production has moved into a California set which previously doubled as another famous fictional Pennsylvania workplace: The Dunder Mifflin Paper Company, otherwise known to millions as the setting of The Office.

Series creator Rob McElhenney, who plays Mac in the show, revealed the new shooting location in a video which he posted to Instagram this week. "Apparently this is Dunder Mifflin, I guess, where they shot The Office," he said, with the building in the background. "I wasn't 100% sure of that. But they're gone now. They're off the air. And now you got..." He then rotated his phone to show Danny DeVito standing in the doorway to his trailer, wearing no pants, talking to Charlie Day. "Sorry, Carell," McElhenney added. "I guess your show got canceled or whatever, but we're still doing ours."

Both The Office and Always Sunny premiered in 2005. But while The Office made a lasting impact on pop culture, running far longer than the British sitcom on which it was based and helping launch the careers of just about everyone who appeared on it, it did go off the air in 2013, while Always Sunny is still going strong.
