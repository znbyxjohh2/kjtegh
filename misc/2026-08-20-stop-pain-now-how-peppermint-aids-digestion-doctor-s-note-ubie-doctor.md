How does peppermint aid in digestion?

Peppermint may support digestion by relaxing the smooth muscles of the stomach and intestines. This relaxing effect can help ease common digestive symptoms such as bloating, gas, cramping, and general stomach discomfort, making it a widely used natural remedy for mild digestive issues.

Explanation

Peppermint oil is often used in gastroenterology to help with digestion. It works by relaxing the muscles in the stomach and intestines. This can make it easier for food to move through the digestive system and may help reduce symptoms like bloating, gas, and stomach pain.
