# Kane Brown Always Has Cake Pops In His Fridge

[Pops In His Fridge](https://benpepsi.com/)

[Kane Brown Always Has](https://7161777.com/cs6m71kh/)

COUNTRY STAR KANE Brown is riding high right now after the release of his latest album, The High Road. Not only has the 31-year-old reached the #7 spot on the US Billboard 200 and #2 on Billboard's Top Country Albums with his latest effort, but he's also in the best shape of his life, and eating with a better diet than he ever has before. To celebrate all of the above, Brown took some time to invite Men's Health into his home for the latest episode of Gym and Fridge.

Brown started the video by taking MH inside his beautiful refrigerator (he's got a great big transparent glass door). He explained that while a few years ago he would've had unhealthy snacks like frozen pizza, Pop Tarts, and soda, nowadays that's all gone (the only kind of soda he ever keeps around is Olipop or Poppi, which offer gut health benefits). He keeps things gluten-free, too. "Everything's really organic, and neat and clean," he says.

One thing the country star always keeps on hand in that transparent refrigerator, though? A delightful snack: cake pops. Think the famous Starbucks-made snack, but with a healthy twist. "I don't know the actual ingredients in here, but I love these things." His wife, Katelyn, then helped fill in some of the ingredients from off-camera: almond butter, oats, chocolate chips. Sweet, yes, but with the beneficial heart-healthy fats that come with almond butter.
