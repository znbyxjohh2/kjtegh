Explanation

For those with PCOS who experience hormonal acne, certain dietary changes might be beneficial. According to the medical literature, including a low-glycemic diet, which means eating foods that don't cause big spikes in blood sugar, can help improve acne. This includes foods like whole grains, fruits, and vegetables.

Additionally, reducing dairy intake might also be helpful, as some studies suggest that dairy products, especially skim milk, can contribute to acne.

Another approach that might be considered is intermittent fasting, which involves eating during specific times and fasting at others. This method has shown some promise in managing weight and insulin levels, which can be beneficial for those with PCOS.
