# Get A Behind-the-Scenes Look At The Rock's New Show

Getting a call from Dwayne "The Rock" Johnson telling you that you've been cast for his new athletic competition show is, understandably, a jarring experience. One competitor on NBC's The Titan Games (premiering January 3) hosted and overseen by Johnson screams into his phone when he gets the news in this exclusive behind-the-scenes look at how the series came together.

Billing itself as the "ultimate athletic competition," Titan Games will pit 64 male and female contestants against each other in a mountain-like structure with some crazy-looking challenges based on Johnson's own workouts and physical achievements. The competitors are normal people who happen to be extremely fit, a requirement to make it through the rigorous setup. They include a metal polisher (the guy losing it when Johnson calls him), MMA fighters, personal trainers, an ICU nurse, and one hell of a dedicated 50-year-old grandmother.
