Glucosamine

One of the most beneficial supplements is Glucosamine, a natural compound derived from shellfish that helps strengthen the cartilage surrounding your knee joints. Glucosamine can also significantly reduce pain, improve the range of motion and reduce swelling in individuals suffering from knee osteoarthritis.

Bromelain

Bromelain is a powerful enzyme derived from pineapple stems that have been used for centuries in traditional medicine to reduce inflammation and improve joint health. This enzyme may be beneficial in helping to rebuild the connective tissue surrounding the joints, particularly in people with knee-cracking problems.

Bromelain helps to break down and remove damaged and unhealthy cells from the joints, which in turn helps reduce inflammation and improve joint health and is also believed to help reduce pain, swelling, and discomfort in the affected area. In addition, bromelain may also help to reduce joint stiffness and improve flexibility.
