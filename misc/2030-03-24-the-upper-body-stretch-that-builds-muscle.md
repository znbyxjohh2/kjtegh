# The Upper-Body Stretch That Builds Muscle

[Upper-Body Stretch That](https://lifegoesom.com/7xbuw2vg/)

By forcing you to hold an exercise movement at its bottom (or hardest) point, Eccentric Quasi-Isometrics (EQIs) stretch your muscles while adding strength where you need it most, says Men’s Health Fitness Director BJ Gaddour, C.S.C.S.

[Upper-Body Stretch That Builds](https://nootropic-atlas.escortskhi.com/blog/5886255580.html)

You can apply EQIs to tons of exercises. But in the video above, Gaddour demonstrates EQIs using dumbbell pullover extensions. It will stretch your lats and triceps, bulletproof your shoulders, and improve muscle activation at that specific range of motion, he says. The upshot: You’ll get better at any exercise where you need to bring your arm over your head, like pullups.
