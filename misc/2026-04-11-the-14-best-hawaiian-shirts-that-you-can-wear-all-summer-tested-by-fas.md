If you’re in the market for a Hawaiian shirt, take into account the following specs to ensure you cop one that meets your style and comfort needs.

Material

When the sun’s beating down, the best Hawaiian shirts should feel lightweight and flowy. That said, stick to finding breathable fabrics like cotton, linen, rayon or even silk. These materials have varying levels of softness, drape, and ability to withstand frequent washing, so choose one that feels as good as it looks, and lasts through all your summer adventures.

Fit

Typically, Hawaiian shirts come in a regular or relaxed fit in a camp collar style. Depending on your preference, you can score one made in a boxy, oversized cut or designed to be more tailored to the body. Whichever you decide, the key is to make sure the fit is breathable and comfortable at all times.
