Patricia Schultz, MSN, APRN, AGNP-C

A lifetime in care: "I’ve worked at Rush University Medical Center since 1986, and while my career has been rooted in primary care, my true passion has always been women’s health. What’s most rewarding about women's health is building relationships that last decades and witnessing how personalized, preventive care can truly transform quality of life. At Midi, I’m excited to continue that mission in a setting designed to truly prioritize women’s midlife health."

Seeing the whole picture: "My philosophy is to understand the complete patient—how every system in the body connects and impacts the challenges women face in midlife. It’s never just about hormones or symptoms in isolation. It’s about lifestyle, stress, sleep, nutrition, and emotional well-being all working together. I take time to listen deeply and help patients connect the dots between what’s happening in their bodies and what they’re feeling day to day."

Not alone in the journey: "Midlife changes happen to all women. I thrive in guiding patients through these phases so they can live their best lives, feeling informed, supported, and never alone. I want every woman to know that there are solutions, and that she deserves to feel good in her own skin. Empowering women with knowledge, compassion, and the right tools isn’t just medicine to me, it’s a partnership that honors their strength and resilience."
