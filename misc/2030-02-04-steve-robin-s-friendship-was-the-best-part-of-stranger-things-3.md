While Stranger Things 3 ended on a very, very sad note, there were some moments that brightened up the season. Who can forget Dustin and Suzie's random sing-off as monsters were trying to take over Hawkins, or Alexei's love of fast food? But none of those moments captured fans' hearts like the friendship between Robin & Steve.

Robin is a new character in the Stranger Things world, and she works with Steve at Scoops Ahoy, an ice cream shop in town's Starcourt Mall. While the two characters seem different at first—Steve the popular high schooler, and Robin being the sarcastic loner—they eventually become closer, and reach BFF status. And, I mean, being held captive and drugged by Russian soldiers would create a bond between anyone.
