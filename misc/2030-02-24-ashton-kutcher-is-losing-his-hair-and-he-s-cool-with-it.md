# Ashton Kutcher Is Losing His Hair — and He's Cool With It

Ashton Kutcher is losing it — his hair, that is.

The 40-year-old actor, who started his career as a model, appeared on Conan Monday to promote his Netflix series, The Ranch. But when host Conan O'Brien complimented him for having a good head of hair, Kutcher shared that he had been experiencing hair loss since the age of 25, and had a bit of a receding hairline because of it. (Hey, it happens to a lot of young guys.)

"I noticed it was starting to go, and so I went and met the hair guy doctor and he put me on this medication called Avodart," he shared. "Your body produces testosterone. The byproduct of testosterone actually causes follicles to get weaker and fall out, so you take this stuff and it washes away the byproduct of testosterone. But, we don't know what else might be used for that could be good in the body."

Kutcher, who married Mila Kunis in 2015 and has two children with the actress, said he got off of the medication when he and Kunis first decided they wanted to start a family.

"I didn't know: Is it safe to take?" he said, explaining his decision. "So, I stopped taking the stuff about three years ago, and now I'm experiencing the hair loss. It's starting to go. I'm a baseball cap guy, so I think I'm OK. I've established myself as a baseball cap wearing individual."
