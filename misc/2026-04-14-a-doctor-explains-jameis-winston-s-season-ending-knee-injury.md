New Orleans Saints quarterback Jameis Winston sustained a major knee injury that fans are worried could be season-ending in a game against the Tampa Bay Bucaneers yesterday. "Apparently Winston has already had an MRI... and the initial concern from the team is an ACL tear and possibly an ACL (anterior cruciate ligament) tear," says Dr. Sutterer. "The mechanism would certainly fit with those possible injuries."

In the footage, you can see Winston's left knee get stuck outward as he's knocked toward the ground, creating a grisly-looking bend as his body falls. "This combination here of his knee falling inward is what we call a knee valgus position," he says. "Which is the knee essentially falling inward here relative to the trunk and the rest of the body. This is common position where we see ACL tears."

Dr. Sutterer also explains that Winston's MCL, PCL, and meniscus could have also sustained injury based on the fall and twist of the knee.
