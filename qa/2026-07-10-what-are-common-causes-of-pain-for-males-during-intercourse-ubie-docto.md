Explanation

Pain during intercourse for males can be due to several factors. Infections such as sexually transmitted infections (STIs) can cause discomfort and pain. Skin disorders like balanitis may also lead to pain during sex. Additionally, chronic pain conditions such as prostatitis or pelvic pain syndrome can be significant contributors.
