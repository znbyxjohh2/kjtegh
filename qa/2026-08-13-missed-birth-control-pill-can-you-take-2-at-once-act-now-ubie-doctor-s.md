Can you take two birth control pills at once if you forgot?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Missed a birth control pill? You can typically take two pills at once to catch up and maintain pregnancy prevention. The exact steps depend on the type of pill (combination vs. progestin-only) and how many hours late you are. If more than 24 hours have passed, backup contraception may be needed for up to 7 days.

When you forget to take your birth control pill, it is often recommended to take the missed pill as soon as you remember. If you miss a pill and do not remember until the next day, taking two pills at once is a common way to get back on track. This approach can help maintain the effectiveness of the birth control in preventing pregnancy.

Steps to Follow:

Missed One Pill: Take the missed pill as soon as you remember. If you remember the next day, take two pills that day.

Missed Two Pills: If you miss two pills in a row, take two pills on the day you remember and two pills the next day.
