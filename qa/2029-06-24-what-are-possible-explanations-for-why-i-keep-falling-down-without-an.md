Explanation

Falling unexpectedly can have several explanations. One common cause is peripheral neuropathy, which affects the nerves in your feet and legs, making it hard to feel the ground properly. This can lead to tripping or losing balance easily.

Another reason might be neurological diseases. These are conditions that affect your brain and nerves, and they can make it difficult to walk or keep your balance.

For older adults, a condition called backward disequilibrium can also lead to falls. This happens when a person leans backward without realizing it, which can cause them to fall.

If you are experiencing frequent falls, it's important to talk to a healthcare provider who can help identify the cause and suggest ways to prevent future falls.
