# Ointment Before or After Moisturizer? Stop This Mistake Now! | Ubie Doctor's Note

Published on: 7/21/2026

Should I apply ointment before or after moisturizer?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Apply ointment after moisturizer for optimal skin protection and hydration. Moisturizer delivers water and active ingredients to the skin first, while ointment acts as an occlusive barrier that seals in moisture and prevents transepidermal water loss. Reversing this order reduces effectiveness, as ointment blocks moisturizer absorption.

Quick order: Cleanse → Moisturizer → Ointment

This layering method is especially helpful for dry, cracked, or irritated skin.

If you're dealing with persistent dryness, itching, rashes, or unexplained skin changes, layering products may only be part of the solution. Skin symptoms can signal a range of conditions—from eczema and dermatitis to something requiring medical attention. When deciding the order of applying moisturizer and ointment, it is important to consider their functions. Moisturizers are designed to add moisture to the skin, while ointments create a barrier to lock in that moisture.

Steps to Follow:

Apply Moisturizer First: Moisturizers help hydrate the skin by adding water and improving skin softness.

Apply Ointment Second: Ointments are thicker and help seal in the moisture provided by the moisturizer, offering a protective barrier.

This order helps ensure that your skin stays hydrated and protected, making it feel softer and healthier. Applying ointment after moisturizer can enhance the skin's ability to retain moisture and protect against external irritants.
