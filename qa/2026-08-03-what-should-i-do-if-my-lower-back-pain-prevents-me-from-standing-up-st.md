Lower back pain that prevents you from standing up straight can be caused by several conditions. One possible cause is flatback syndrome, which can happen after spinal surgery. This condition makes it hard to stand up straight because the spine loses its normal curve. Another possible cause is a herniated disc in the lower back. This can cause pain not only in the back but also in areas like the groin.

What You Can Do:

Consult a Healthcare Provider: It's important to see a healthcare provider to find out the exact cause of your pain.

Diagnosis: The provider may use imaging tests to see what's happening with your spine.
