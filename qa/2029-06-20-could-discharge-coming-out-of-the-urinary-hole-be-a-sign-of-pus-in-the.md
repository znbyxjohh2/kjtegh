Explanation

Discharge from the urinary hole, or urethral discharge, can sometimes be a sign of pus in the urethra. This can occur due to infections. One possible cause of such discharge is herpes simplex virus (HSV) infection.

Urethral Discharge: Urethral discharge can be linked to various medical conditions.

Herpes Simplex Virus: References 2 and 3 highlight that herpes simplex virus can cause genital secretions and urethritis, which is inflammation of the urethra. This inflammation can lead to discharge, which might contain pus.

In summary, if you notice discharge from the urinary hole, it can be due to an infection like herpes, which can cause urethritis and lead to pus in the urethra. It's important to consult with a healthcare provider for an accurate diagnosis and appropriate treatment.
