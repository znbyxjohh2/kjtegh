What Happens If You Take Viagra Too Often?

Using Viagra more than directed won’t help you improve performance in the bedroom. In fact, it can actually increase risk. Sildenafil side effects are usually mild when taken as prescribed, but overuse can make them more intense or potentially dangerous.

Taking Viagra too often can also lower your blood pressure more than expected, lead to priapism (a prolonged, painful erection), or stress your cardiovascular system, especially if you have heart disease.
