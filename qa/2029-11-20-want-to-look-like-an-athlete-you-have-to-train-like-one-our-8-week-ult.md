IF YOU WANT to be as fit and healthy as possible for as long as possible, then you need to think of yourself as an athlete.

No, that doesn't mean you're suiting up for the Packers on Sundays, or even planning to dunk a basketball. But training and building athleticism just may be the missing piece to you living your best life. Yes, muscle and strength are big, but a well-rounded athletic program adds bounce and youth to every step you take—in addition to helping you avoid injuries and setting you up to dominate games of tag and catch and backyard football with the kids. On top of that, a smart athletic training program still sets you up to build more muscle, shed pounds, and supercharge your overall fitness.
