Explanation

Abdominal pain can have many causes, and understanding them can help you manage the pain better.

1. Stress: Stress can cause or worsen abdominal pain. When you are stressed, your body might react by making your stomach hurt. If you think stress is a problem, talking to someone or finding ways to relax can help. For more help with stress, visit 988lifeline.org.

2. Systemic Causes: Sometimes, problems in other parts of your body can cause your stomach to hurt. This is called a systemic cause. For example, issues with your heart or lungs might make your belly hurt.

3. Chronic Conditions: Chronic abdominal pain means your stomach hurts for a long time. This can be due to conditions like irritable bowel syndrome (IBS) or other digestive problems. If your stomach hurts a lot and it doesn't go away, it's good to talk to a doctor.
