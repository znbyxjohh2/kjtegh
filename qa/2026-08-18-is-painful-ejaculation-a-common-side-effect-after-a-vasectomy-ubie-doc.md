Is painful ejaculation a common side effect after a vasectomy?

Painful ejaculation is not a common side effect after a vasectomy, but it can happen to some men.

Explanation

After a vasectomy, some men might experience pain during ejaculation, but it is not very common. Most men do not have this problem.

Possible Causes:

According to the medical literature, some men might feel pain due to pressure build-up or inflammation.

In some cases, nerves might be affected during the procedure.

What to Do:

If someone experiences pain, they should talk to their doctor. There are treatments available.

Sometimes, a vasectomy reversal can help with pain issues, but this is usually considered when other treatments don't work.

Overall, while painful ejaculation can happen, it is not something most men will experience after a vasectomy.
