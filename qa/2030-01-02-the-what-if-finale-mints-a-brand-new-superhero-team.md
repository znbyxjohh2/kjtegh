Warning: This article contains spoilers for the finale of What If…? Season 1.

At the end of the first season of Marvel’s animated series What If…?, Uatu the Watcher commissions some of the most powerful superheroes in the galaxy for the ultimate team up. No, it’s not the Guardians of the Galaxy he calls, or the Eternals. He crafts a new team from universes we’ve only briefly explored through his eyes—throughout the episodes of Season 1 of What If...?—and names them the Guardians of the Multiverse.

[What If Finale Mints](https://biopeptide.wiki/)

Consisting of Captain Carter, Gamora, Star-Lord T’Challa, Killmonger, Supreme Dr. Strange, Black Widow, and Party Thor, the new supergroup combines every hero made from the previous What If…? episodes as they try to take on an Infinity Stone powered Ultron.

The team manages to take him down, but then Killmonger decides to take the Infinity Stones for himself. A fight ensues between Ultron (now taken over by an Arnim Zola AI) and Killmonger. To avoid further chaos, Supreme Doctor Strange traps the two in the pocket universe he found himself in after losing control at the end of his earlier episode, sacrificing his own existence to make sure these villains don't wreak further havoc upon the multiverse. The rest of the Guardians are sent back to their timelines, back to the moments just as the Watcher called upon them. So, in a way, the Guardians were disbanded as quickly as they were formed.

With any new MCU introduction, there’s one question that’ll always be on the mind of any Marvel fan who hasn’t jumped into the source material yet: Is it in the comics? While the answer for the Guardians may be disappointing to anyone looking for more adventures with this superhero group, never fear, there’s a similar group that serves the same purpose. And they probably served as the inspiration for the group featured in the animated series.

Are the Guardians of the Multiverse in the Marvel Comics?

No, the Guardians are not in the comics. It was an invention created for the show, and likely a play on the Guardians of the Galaxy. And well, all the other various groups Marvel Comics has that have yet to be introduced into the MCU (which also includes groups you may not have heard of, like the A-Force, along with some obvious ones on the way, like the Fantastic Four and the X-Men).

Still, viewers may be interested to learn more about a similar group called the Council of the Guardians of All Galaxies.

What is the Council of the Guardians of All Galaxies?

Marvel Guardians of the Galaxy by Abnett & Lanning: The Complete Collection Volume 2

Made up of various iterations of the Guardians of the Galaxy, the Council first appeared in Guardians of the Galaxy (Vol. 2) #25 back in 2010. The group serves as an introduction to a flashback about the Guardians of the Galaxy, who fight Thanos in a log the Council then reads in the year 3000.

The Council isn’t made of the Guardians we know, but features a number of Marvel characters yet to be seen in the MCU, including Ghost Rider and Charlie-27.

Although there’s nothing quite like Captain Carter, Party Thor, and others teaming up and breaking out of their timelines, there’s a ton of Marvel supergroups yet to be explored, and may even show up in the next phase of the MCU.
