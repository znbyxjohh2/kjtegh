# Q: I have long legs. What should I wear?

Thanks for writing. Simply asking the question, we know you get it. It’s about proportion — one of the trickier elements of looking great, but essential. The goal is to achieve balance in everything you wear. Loose with fitted, short with long, dark with light, etc. Creating balance for you, Mr. Longlegs, is a cinch.

Long on Top Your big trick: Wear jackets and sweaters that extend slightly lower than the norm. The trend in suit and sport coats is fitted and shorter, with the bottom of the jacket just skimming your butt. You should look for something about an inch or two longer. Ask the salesperson which brands cut their coats a little longer. Ditto for shirts worn untucked and sweaters. Ones that hit you a few inches below your belt loops will extend you’re the length of your torso.

Contrast is Good Wear colors that contrast on your top and bottom half (a light shirt and dark pants, for example). This will de-emphasize longer legs. Monochromatic outfits make you look taller, which isn’t your aim here.

Shorten Your Legs (Visually) Feel free to wear lower-rise jeans or add cuffs to your trousers — two big no-no’s for shorter guys. With shorts and swim trunks, longer is better. Pick styles that hit you at or just above the knee. And where we’d advise short guys to wear shoes that match their pants to create a longer visual line, you should wear a shoe that contrasts with your pants, when it works with the outfit. This will create the impression of slightly shorter legs. If you’re wearing a patterned suit, opt for a windowpane or check rather than pinstripes, which makes your legs look longer.
