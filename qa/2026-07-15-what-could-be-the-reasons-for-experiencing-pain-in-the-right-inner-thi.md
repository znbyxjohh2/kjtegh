Pain in the right inner thigh can have several causes. Nerve-related issues are one possibility. The obturator nerve, which passes through the thigh, can sometimes cause pain if it is irritated or damaged. This nerve issue might require specific treatments to help manage the pain.

Injuries or strains in the muscles around the thigh are another common cause. This can happen from physical activities or accidents. Muscle strains usually need rest and sometimes physical therapy to heal.

Other medical conditions might also lead to thigh pain. For example, certain rare conditions can affect the thigh area and need special medical attention.
