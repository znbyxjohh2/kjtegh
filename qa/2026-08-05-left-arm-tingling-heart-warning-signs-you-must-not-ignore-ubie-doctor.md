Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Left arm tingling can be a warning sign of heart problems, particularly when it occurs alongside chest tightness, pressure, or pain. This combination of symptoms may indicate a heart attack or other cardiovascular issue and requires immediate medical attention. However, left arm tingling can also stem from non-cardiac causes, such as pinched nerves, poor circulation, anxiety, or a herniated disc in the neck.
