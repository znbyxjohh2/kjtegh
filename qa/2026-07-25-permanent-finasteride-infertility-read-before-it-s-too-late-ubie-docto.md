# Permanent Finasteride Infertility? Read Before It’s Too Late | Ubie Doctor's Note

Published on: 7/15/2026

Will finasteride make me infertile even if I stop taking it?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Finasteride can temporarily affect male fertility during use by lowering sperm count, reducing semen volume, and impacting sperm motility. In most cases, these effects are reversible, and fertility typically returns to normal within months after stopping the medication. Men trying to conceive should consult a healthcare provider about pausing treatment.

Finasteride is a medication that can affect male fertility by reducing sperm count and quality. However, the effects are usually not permanent.

Key Points:

Temporary Effects: While taking finasteride, some men might experience changes in sperm production, leading to reduced fertility.

Reversibility: According to the studies, once you stop taking finasteride, these effects are often reversible, and fertility can improve over time.

Individual Variability: The degree to which finasteride affects fertility can vary from person to person. Some may see more significant effects than others.
