About the Symptom

Swelling, redness, and pain located where urine comes out due to infection or inflammation.

When to See a Doctor

Seek professional care if you experience any of the following symptoms

Sensation of incomplete voiding

Abdominal pain

Possible Causes

Generally, Redness or swelling of the urethra can be related to:

Gonorrhea is a sexually transmitted disease caused by the N. gonorrhoeae bacteria.

Infection of the vagina by a parasite called Trichomonas. The disease is sexually transmitted but often does not cause any symptoms in men.

A sexually transmitted infectious disease. It can cause abnormal vaginal or penile discharge and pain in the pubic area, but most infections have no symptoms. Chlamydia infection can also cause infertility and ectopic pregnancies and can be transmitted to the throat and rectum.

Cystitis

Related Serious Diseases

Sometimes, Redness or swelling of the urethra may be related to these serious diseases:

Bacterial infection of the urethra (tube from which urine exits the body) leading to pain and penile discharge. It is most often caused by sexually transmitted diseases like Gonorrhea and Chlamydia infections as well as viruses like herpes.

Doctor's Diagnostic Questions

Your doctor may ask these questions to check for this symptom:

Is there redness or swelling around the urethral opening?

How long have you had redness or swelling around the urethral opening?

Have you experienced redness or swelling around the urethral opening before?

How has the redness or swelling around the urethral opening changed since it started?
